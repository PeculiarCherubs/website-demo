# Publications page refinement

The Publications page had accumulated two overlapping presentation models.

## Problems corrected
- Removed the generic Featured Publication block that was surfacing an older Goodnews post.
- Morning Devotion is now the first publication experience after the hero.
- `Remembered by Mercy` remains the newest devotion and opens through:
  `publication.html?slug=remembered-by-mercy`
- Goodnews and Sunday School are presented together as secondary highlights.
- The searchable/filterable publication library now has the real anchor:
  `#publication-archive`
- “Browse devotions / Goodnews / lessons” links now scroll to the archive and activate the correct filter.
- Lectionary remains its own final section.

## New page hierarchy
1. Publications hero
2. Daily Morning Devotion
3. Goodnews + Sunday School highlights
4. Searchable Publication Library
5. Calendar of Lectionary
